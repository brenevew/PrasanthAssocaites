## Why

1. **"Builders" in Coimbatore mostly means developers.** The listings that rank sell flats and plots, and several have 200–400 reviews. That search is the hardest one for a design-and-build firm to win.
2. **Reviews.** The Coimbatore listing has 32 reviews. The top builders have 80–400.
3. **Domain age.** The site is two months old, while the directories and older builders have years of links.
4. **Wording.** The Coimbatore page never used "builders in Coimbatore" in its text; it targeted "building contractors" only.

Where the listing does appear (civil engineers, architects and engineers), it matches what the business is.
