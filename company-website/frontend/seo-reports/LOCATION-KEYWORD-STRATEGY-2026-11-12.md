# Services × Locations SEO: Keywords, Competitors and Changes

Date: 2026-11-12. Scope: prasanthassociates.com, all 22 services, across Coimbatore, Ooty, Coonoor, Kotagiri and Gudalur. Status: implemented and verified on a local build; **not deployed**.

## How the keywords were chosen

- **Google autocomplete** (India, English, 2026-11-12): about 100 seed phrases, such as "builders in ooty" and "structural engineer coimbatore". A phrase Google suggests is one people really type. Autocomplete gives no volumes.
- **Competitor pages**: titles, headings, meta keywords and page lists read directly from each site.
- **Not available**: search volumes, keyword difficulty and your current rankings. Search Console and keyword tools aren't connected, and Google's results pages block automated reading. Nothing below should be read as a volume.
