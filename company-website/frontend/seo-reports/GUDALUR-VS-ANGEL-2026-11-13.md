## 3. What only you can do (this decides Maps)

In order of effect:

1. **Get fresh reviews on the Gudalur listing, every month.** This is the biggest gap. Ask every current and recent Gudalur client. A message you can send on WhatsApp:

   > Thank you for building with Prasanth Associates. Could you spare two minutes to review us on Google? It helps other families in Gudalur find us. If you can, mention what we built for you and where. [your review link]

   Asking clients to describe the work is allowed. Don't offer anything in return, and send it to every client, not only the happy ones. Aim for 3–4 new reviews a month.

2. **Reply to all 45 existing reviews**, starting with the newest. A sentence or two each, naming the work where you know it ("Glad the house at … turned out well").

3. **Fill in the listing's Services section** (Google Business Profile → Edit services). Add each of these as a service with a one-line description: House construction · Building contractor · Villa and bungalow construction · Commercial building construction · Renovation · Interior design · Modular kitchens · House plans and plan approval · Structural drawings · Estimates and BoQ · Bank valuation reports. Google matches these to searches.

4. **Update the business description** (750 characters allowed). Draft:

   > Prasanth Associates is a construction company in Gudalur, The Nilgiris. Our builders and civil engineers design and build houses, estate bungalows and commercial buildings, and handle renovation, interior design, house plans, structural drawings, estimates and valuation reports. Completed work in Gudalur includes the Pricol Limited tea bungalow, Sasthapuri Hotels and family homes. Every project is priced through an itemised BoQ before work starts. Visit our branch on Ooty Main Road, near the SBI ATM, Monday to Saturday, 9 am to 6 pm.

5. **Post on the listing every week or two**: one site photo with two lines about the stage of work. Your listing shows no posts; Greenwood's does.

6. **Add photos regularly.** You have 44, Angel has 58. Upload the project photos already on the website, plus new site photos as work progresses.

7. **Fix the Tamil name** on the listing, which still reads "Prasanth Construction" in Tamil script.

8. **Do not add keywords to the business name.** It's against Google's rules and can get the listing suspended. If a competitor's name is padded with keywords that aren't part of its real name, anyone can report it through "Suggest an edit" on that listing; that's your choice to make.

9. **Local links to the website:** a listing on Justdial and Sulekha under Gudalur, the Gudalur traders' or builders' association if one lists members, and suppliers or clients willing to link (for example, a client's site mentioning who built their building). Angel's 8-year-old domain has had time to collect these.

## 4. What to expect

- **Website changes:** Google usually recrawls within days once you request indexing, but a 2-month-old domain earns trust slowly. Expect gradual movement over 2–4 months, not days.
- **Maps:** review freshness and listing activity can move positions within weeks.
- **Angel's advantages that remain:** domain age and the word "Constructions" in its name. Neither can be copied; both can be outweighed by reviews, activity and better content.
