## 9. Limitations

- No geo-grid: rankings were read once from the centre of each area, not across a grid of points. A 7×7 grid over Coimbatore would show where the listing does and doesn't appear (needs DataForSEO, or a paid tool such as Local Falcon or BrightLocal).
- Review dates come from the reviews Google featured, not a full history.
- OpenStreetMap competitor counts couldn't be run (servers overloaded), and OSM coverage of Indian builders is thin anyway. Google Maps results were used for competitors instead.
- Apple Maps has no public lookup.
- Profile dashboards (secondary categories, services, Insights, calls, direction requests) are only visible to you.
