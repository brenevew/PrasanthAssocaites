# Competitor and search-intent analysis

Date: 21 November 2026.

**Sources and limits.** This file combines (a) web searches run on 21 November with a general search tool, which is not Google India and has no location set, so they show *who publishes pages for these searches*, not Google India rankings; (b) the Google Maps spot checks from 13 November in `frontend/seo-reports/COIMBATORE-BUILDERS-2026-11-13.md` and `frontend/seo-reports/GUDALUR-VS-ANGEL-2026-11-13.md`; (c) Search Console. No competitor traffic, rankings, backlink counts or keyword volumes are estimated. Where a figure appears, it was read from the cited public page on that date.

## 1. What the results look like

For every non-branded search tested on 21 November, the organic results were dominated by **directories and marketplaces**, not builders' own websites:

| Search | What appeared |
|---|---|
| civil contractors in Coimbatore house construction | IndiaMART seller pages (Infinix Infrastructure, Subam Construction, Cellarium Construction, AR Constructions, Seyon Consociate), Houzz profiles (Vinayaga Buildings, Structs & Designs), a CG Infra profile, Architizer (Jeevan Build Tech) |
| villa construction company Coimbatore | IndiaMART (Cellarium, Usha Constructions, AR Constructions), BankBazaar and Housing.com builder lists, CommonFloor, Kanish Homes' own site |
| construction company in Ooty builders | RealEstateIndia directory pages for Ooty, Coonoor and Kotagiri; IndiaMART (Velankani Constructions, Alpine Meadows); Dun & Bradstreet listings; CommonFloor; McCoy Mart |
| builders in Gudalur Nilgiris | Dun & Bradstreet listings (Olivia Builders, Promatrix, Homeart, and others); InstaFinancials (Gudalur Hightech Builders Pvt Ltd, incorporated 2005); Housing.com; IndiaMART (K.R.S Builders, Kotagiri) |
| construction company Coonoor house builders | IndiaMART (Anugrahas Infra, J And S Construction, Hill Top Construction, Foundations Builders & Developers); Vitrag Group's own site; Houzz (Bezalel Architects) |

**Implications:**

1. On these searches the site competes with directory pages more than with competitors' websites. A listing on those directories is how a builder appears *inside* those results. The site has no listing on IndiaMART, Justdial or Sulekha (13 Nov audit). That is the largest gap a competitor does not have, and it is an owner action (backlog A3).
2. Several IndiaMART sellers publish a square-foot price (₹1,700–₹2,200 per sq. ft. for villas or houses, as listed by sellers on 21 Nov). The site deliberately publishes no rate, and the owner has not confirmed the calculator rates (backlog A5). The site's BoQ explanation is the honest alternative, and the change log does not add any price.
3. In the Nilgiris district, almost no builder has a substantial website. Pages with documented local projects (Rex school, Pricol, Sasthapuri, Manjushree) are an advantage that directory pages cannot copy.

### Ooty, Coonoor, Kotagiri

No builder website stood out. Results were directories, plus Vitrag Group (Coonoor, "20+ homes since 2011", per its own site) and Foundations Builders & Developers (Coonoor, "nearly twenty five years", per its IndiaMART page). Search Console shows "ooty builders" at position 8 and "construction company in ooty" at 57. Prasanth has no Ooty office, so a Maps listing there is not possible; organic content and directory listings are the route.
