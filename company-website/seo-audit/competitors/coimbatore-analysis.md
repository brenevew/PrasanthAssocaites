## Gaps for Prasanth Associates

1. **Review count.** 32 against 50–400 for the businesses that rank. Biggest single gap. (Owner action)
2. **Category fit.** "Builders" in Coimbatore is dominated by apartment and plot developers. Construction, contractor and engineering searches are more winnable. (Strategy)
3. **Prices.** Two website competitors publish rates; cost searches are common. (Needs owner-confirmed rates)
4. **Directories.** No Justdial, Sulekha or IndiaMART listing found. (Owner action)
5. **Engineering strength.** S G Structural Engineers and Vikruti compete on engineering; your structural, stability-certificate and valuation pages are an advantage most builders lack.
