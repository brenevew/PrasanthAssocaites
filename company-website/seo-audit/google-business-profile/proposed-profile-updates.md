> Prasanth Associates is a construction company in Coimbatore, with its head office in Gandhipuram on Sathy Road. Our builders and civil engineers design and build houses, villas and commercial buildings, and handle renovation, house plans, building plan approval, structural drawings, building estimates, stability certificates and bank valuation reports. Every construction project is priced through an itemised BoQ before work starts. Visit us Monday to Saturday, 9 am to 6 pm, or call before you come so an engineer is free to meet you.
